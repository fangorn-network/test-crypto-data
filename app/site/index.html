Crypto Market Tape

Recorded market data for Polymarket US crypto up/down markets (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP; 5-minute, 15-minute and 1-hour windows): every trade and every top-10 order-book change, plus Coinbase spot top-of-book once a second. One file of each per UTC day, published to Fangorn, where each is signed and anchored on chain. Recording runs part of each day; the timeline shows the span covered.
